<h1>Homework 15: Torque Wrench Design Project</h1>

<h2>5.1 The Baseline Design</h2>

<h3>5.1.1 Results</h3>

<h4>1. Results from Hand Calculations of the Baseline Design</h4>

<p>
The baseline design consists of a non-ratcheting,
3/8-inch-drive torque wrench rated for 600 in-lbf.
The wrench is modeled as a cantilever beam with a
rectangular cross-section subjected to a transverse
force at the end of the handle.
</p>

<p>
The objective is to determine the maximum normal
stress and load-point deflection using analytical
beam equations.
</p>

<h4>Given Parameters</h4>

<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Parameter</th>
<th>Symbol</th>
<th>Value</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Material</td><td>—</td><td>M42 Steel</td></tr>
<tr><td>Applied torque</td><td>T</td><td>600 in-lbf</td></tr>
<tr><td>Handle length</td><td>L</td><td>16 in</td></tr>
<tr><td>Width</td><td>h</td><td>0.45 in</td></tr>
<tr><td>Thickness</td><td>b</td><td>0.50 in</td></tr>
<tr><td>Young's modulus</td><td>E</td><td>32 × 10⁶ psi</td></tr>
<tr><td>Poisson's ratio</td><td>ν</td><td>0.29</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h4>Applied Force</h4>

<p>
The applied force is calculated from the rated torque
and the distance from the drive to the load point.
</p>

<div class="equation">
T = FL
<br>
F = T / L
<br>
F = 600 / 16
<br>
<span class="result">F = 37.5 lbf</span>
</div>

<h4>Maximum Normal Stress</h4>

<p>
The maximum bending moment occurs at the fixed
end of the cantilever beam.
</p>

<div class="equation">
M<sub>max</sub> = FL
<br>
M<sub>max</sub> = 37.5 × 16
<br>
<span class="result">M<sub>max</sub> = 600 in-lbf</span>
</div>

<p>
For a rectangular cross-section, the second
moment of area is:
</p>

<div class="equation">
I = bh<sup>3</sup> / 12
<br>
I = (0.50 × 0.45<sup>3</sup>) / 12
<br>
<span class="result">I = 0.0037969 in<sup>4</sup></span>
</div>

<p>
Using the flexure formula, the maximum normal
stress is:
</p>

<div class="equation">
σ<sub>max</sub> = M<sub>max</sub>c / I
<br>
c = h / 2 = 0.225 in
<br>
σ<sub>max</sub> = (600 × 0.225) / 0.0037969
<br>
<span class="result">σ<sub>max</sub> = 35.56 ksi</span>
</div>

<h4>Load-Point Deflection</h4>

<p>
The load-point deflection is calculated using
the cantilever beam deflection equation for
an end load.
</p>

<div class="equation">
δ = FL<sup>3</sup> / (3EI)
<br>
δ = (37.5 × 16<sup>3</sup>) /
(3 × 32,000,000 × 0.0037969)
<br>
<span class="result">δ = 0.421 in</span>
</div>

<p>
The calculated maximum normal stress and
load-point deflection agree with the reference
values provided in the assignment.
</p>

<p>
The baseline design does not satisfy the minimum
deflection requirement of 1 inch at the rated
torque of 600 in-lbf. Therefore, modifications
to the material and/or geometry are necessary
to improve the sensitivity of the torque wrench.
</p>
